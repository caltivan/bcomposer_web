BCOMPOSER METRONOME

Visual Rhythm Metronome

Your perfect practice partner to develop precision across three dimensions: visually, audibly, and kinesthetically. Featuring our unique Rhythm Circle indicator, customizable accents, vibration pulse, and an expansive tempo range from 20 to 400 BPM.

Circular Visual Beat

Intuitive circular pulse animation lets you track every beat and subdivision visually—stay in perfect sync even when muted.

20 to 400 BPM & Tap

Fine-tune your exact tempo across a wide range (20–400 BPM) or quickly tap to find and lock in your practice rhythm.

Beat Subdivisions

Practice with precision using quarter, dotted quarter, eighth, sixteenth, 32nd, and 64th note subdivisions.

Extensive Time Signatures

Supports standard, compound, and odd meters: 2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 5/4, 6/8, 7/8, 9/8, 12/8, all the way up to 23/4 and 23/8.

Custom Accents & Pulse

Easily configure custom accents on any beat, with built-in pulse vibration feedback for a true physical sensation of the tempo.

Sounds & Volume Control

Choose between multiple clean tick sounds and manage dedicated volume controls tailored for practice, dance, or stage.
